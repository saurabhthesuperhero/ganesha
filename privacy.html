Privacy Policy

Ganesha Puzzle Adventure (Android game)

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · हिंदी सारांश

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • We, the developer, do not collect personal data. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. With your consent where the law requires it, ads may be personalised using your device's advertising ID.
  • Purchases are optional and handled entirely by Google Play.
  • The game is made for players aged 13 and over.

Who we are

Ganesha Puzzle Adventure (“the game”, “we”, “us”, package com.ganeshapuzzle.game) is a story puzzle game for Android published by the developer shown on its Google Play page. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com.

Information saved on your device

The game has no login and no servers of its own. Everything it needs to remember is stored only on your device:

  • level progress, stars, modaks (in-game coins), hints and unlocked items;
  • Ganpati Pandal decorations, the murti you sculpt and paint, Ganeshotsav event progress, Katha Album cards, Daily Puzzle streak, achievements and daily rewards;
  • settings such as language (English / हिंदी), music, sound, vibration, rhythm timing, tilt controls and daily reminders.

We never receive this data. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include it in your encrypted device backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads (a banner on the map, occasional full-screen ads between levels and optional “watch a video” rewards) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK by Google LLC. To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise them, the SDK may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (the game declares the AD_ID permission for this);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (city-level) location;
  • device and app information such as device model, Android version, language and game version;
  • ad interactions such as impressions and taps, plus diagnostic data from the ads SDK.

This data is collected and used by Google and its ad partners, not by us. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the AdMob data disclosure.

Consent and your choices

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the game shows Google’s consent message (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. If you do not consent, you still get ads, but they are not personalised.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy options.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device). After deleting it, ads are no longer personalised with it.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional. The VIP pass (and the 7 VIP days in the Starter Pack) turns off banners and full-screen ads.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (a Starter Pack, hints, modaks and a monthly or yearly VIP pass). They are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details; Google Play handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. The game only receives a confirmation of what was bought, so it can unlock the item on your device. You can manage or cancel the VIP subscription in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

Other permissions and features

  • Internet / network state: only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. Puzzles play offline.
  • Notifications: optional reminders created on your device (Daily Puzzle, Pandal and Ganeshotsav; switch them off in Settings). No message is sent from a server.
  • Vibration: short haptic feedback, can be turned off in Settings.
  • Motion sensors: some puzzles (such as the tilt mazes) ask you to tilt or shake the phone. Sensor readings are used live on the device and never stored or sent. An on-screen joystick works too.
  • Sharing: when you share a puzzle or a story card, Android's share sheet opens and you choose the app; we receive nothing.

Children

The game is designed for and listed to players aged 13 and over; it is not directed at children under 13 and the ads are not marked as child-directed. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (maximum ad content rating PG). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game and data was collected through ads, contact us and we will help, or reset the device's advertising ID as described above.

Data security and retention

Because we keep no data on any server, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Your on-device data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google’s policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Indian DPDP Act) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Since we hold no personal data, the quickest way is to use Settings → Privacy options in the game and your device's ad settings; for anything else, e-mail us.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.
